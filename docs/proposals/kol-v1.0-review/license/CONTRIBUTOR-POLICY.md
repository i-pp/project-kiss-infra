# Contributor licensing policy candidate

**Status: unadopted private review candidate.** This is proposed review procedure, not an executed CLA, assignment, or agreement. Existing contributions are not relicensed by this document.

## Contributor rights

Contributors retain their copyright. KOL requires no copyright assignment and no obligation to submit changes. An offer to contribute under KOL must come from the rightsholder or a person authorized to license the material and any patent rights within the candidate's scope. A GitHub username, repository role, AI tool, or employer affiliation is not proof of that authority.

Before accepting material, identify the actual rightsholder, existing license, contribution source, and relevant employment or contractor restrictions. Preserve existing copyright and license notices. Do not replace a contributor's copyright with `I-plusplus Inc.` unless supported by actual rights evidence.

## New contributions after adoption

Only after Owner has approved adoption and the published contribution process, a submission may use a clear inbound-equals-outbound process. Present the exact license text and the scope of the contribution choice before asking the contributor to record agreement. Preserve the immutable text or digest and the submission record.

Proposed acknowledgement for later approval:

```text
I offer the material identified in this submission for inclusion in the
identified Work under KISS Open License Version 1.0. I hold the rights needed
to make that offer, or have authority from the relevant rightsholder. I have
identified separately licensed material and preserved its notices.
```

This statement is a proposed intake record, not a substitute for a complete CLA or for confirming rights. It grants no authority the submitter lacks and must not be silently added to past submissions. Do not ask a contributor to promise ownership of third-party material or to license third-party patents.

A DCO sign-off, a CLA, a copyright assignment, and KOL's license grant are different instruments. Choose any additional instrument only after review and Owner approval. A DCO alone is not proof that historical material can be relicensed, and a sign-off on another project's contribution is not a KOL grant.

## Existing work and separately licensed contributions

Maintain a rights ledger by repository revision and path. Record rightsholders, original notices, contributor authorization or existing license, the proposed scope, and the decision. For existing material without a sufficient grant, seek the required permission through a separately authorized communication, keep its current license, exclude it, or replace it through a lawful independently reviewed process. Do not assume silence is permission.

If a contribution is offered on different terms, flag it before acceptance. Do not relabel it KOL automatically. Owner must decide whether to accept the material with its original terms, seek a compatible separate grant, or decline it. Counsel reviews any combination affecting license compatibility.

## AI-assisted work

AI assistance does not establish authorship, ownership, provenance, patent permission, or lack of copied code. Review the submitted material, applicable tool terms, input rights, and identifiable third-party material. Record relevant provenance without storing confidential prompts or unnecessary personal information. Apply the same technical, security, ASCII-source, and editorial checks as for other contributions.

## Review record

For an accepted contribution, retain the exact source revision, contributor and rightsholder identification to the extent needed, licensing acknowledgement and text identity, third-party declarations, rights evidence, reviewer, Owner decision when required, and resulting file scope. Do not expose private employment agreements or identity information in a public repository. Resolve what can be published separately.
