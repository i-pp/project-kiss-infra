# Drafting review

**Outcome: review pack prepared; adoption blocked pending Owner and qualified legal review.** This is a text and policy consistency review, not a legal opinion, an OSI or FSF assessment, or a determination of license compatibility or enforceability.

## Review performed

The English candidate, corresponding eleven-section Japanese reference, operational policies, Apache comparison, and Owner decision register were reviewed for consistency with the requested permissive-use direction. An independent drafting and scope review and English-Japanese parity review also considered redistribution, Object-only changes, contributor authority, patent retaliation, protected embedded output material, retained notices, and third-party rights.

The Japanese reference was compared for consequential semantic differences, including the copyright grant, attribution placement, patent termination, tool-output boundary, disclaimer, liability, and language/version clauses. No clear material mismatch was identified in that review. This is not a certified translation.

## Findings addressed

- The grantor is now the rights-holding principal acting through an authorized representative, rather than apparently only the individual who submits a contribution. Each copyright grant is limited to rights the Contributor owns or has authority to license.
- Included output material is governed only where permission under KOL is needed. The text preserves independently available legal rights and separate grants, without creating a runtime exception.
- The Apache comparison explicitly identifies KOL's lack of Apache section 9's express indemnity/defense requirement for downstream undertakings.
- The comparison does not portray the lack of a general cure provision as an omission unique to KOL; Apache also does not state a general cure mechanism.
- The policies distinguish text adoption, application to identified material, and publication. No gate is represented as completed.

No new consequential conflict between the revised English text, Japanese reference and policies was identified in the final drafting pass. That statement does not resolve the decisions below.

## Findings remaining open

- The candidate does not fully define the effect of a non-patent breach, notice and cure, reinstatement, downstream grant survival, or post-termination obligations. These are explicit pre-adoption decisions.
- Patent retaliation has no defensive-filing exception or restoration process. Its scope, timing and future acquired rights need review.
- The no-credit tool-use rule does not exempt protected copied runtime, helper or template material. A broader output exception is an optional Owner choice beyond the tool-use-only rule, not a defect in that rule; it is not drafted or approved.
- Actual rightsholders, corporate identity, existing contribution authority, and file-by-file licensing scope are not verified by drafting review.
- Warranty and liability wording, mandatory law, governing-law and venue choices, and license-text stewardship need qualified legal review.
- Compatibility and recognition must be assessed independently for the exact new license and actual combinations. No conclusion transfers from Apache-2.0.

## Packaging verification

The manifest records file sizes, SHA-256 digests and selected drafting assertions, including the seven requested file names, matching eleven-section structures, ASCII English instrument, exact interim LicenseRef spelling, visible draft status, no operative LICENSES installation, preserved conditional header template, and absence of internal citation tokens. These checks do not establish rights, completeness of a release inventory, semantic equivalence, or legal sufficiency.

The candidate contains review metadata and delimiters. The operative instrument must be separately approved and extracted before any later installation or publication. No adoption or public action was performed.
